## What hydrogen is

Hydrogen is the lightest chemical element. Hydrogen gas does not release carbon dioxide when it is used
in a fuel cell or burned; the main product is water.

## How green hydrogen is made

Green hydrogen is hydrogen produced by electrolysis of water using electricity from renewable sources such
as wind or solar power. Electrolysis uses an electric current to split water into hydrogen and oxygen.
The device that does this is called an electrolyser.

## Other kinds of hydrogen

Most hydrogen today is made from natural gas, which releases carbon dioxide. This is often called grey
hydrogen. When the carbon dioxide from that process is captured and stored, it is often called blue hydrogen.

## Where it can be used

Green hydrogen can be used in industries that are hard to run directly on electricity, such as making steel
and fertiliser. It can also store renewable energy for later use and power fuel cell vehicles.

## Challenges

Green hydrogen is currently more expensive to produce than hydrogen made from natural gas. Electrolysis
loses some energy, so it is less efficient than using renewable electricity directly. Hydrogen also needs
special pipelines and tanks for storage and transport.
