## What procrastination is

Procrastination is voluntarily delaying an intended task even though you expect the delay to make things worse.
It is different from planned rest or deliberate prioritising.

## Why people procrastinate

Many researchers describe procrastination as a problem of managing emotions rather than managing time.
People often delay tasks that feel boring, confusing or stressful, because avoiding the task brings
short-term relief from those feelings.

Rewards that arrive now tend to feel more valuable than rewards that arrive later. This makes a distant
deadline feel less urgent than an enjoyable activity available right now.

Perfectionism and fear of failure can also lead to delay, because starting a task means it can be judged.

## What helps

Breaking a task into a very small first step makes it easier to start.
Removing distractions, such as putting the phone in another room, reduces the pull of easier activities.
Being kind to yourself after procrastinating, instead of harsh self-criticism, is linked with procrastinating
less on the next task.
